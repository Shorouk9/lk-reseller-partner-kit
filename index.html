01 · Who to recruit

Ideal partner profile

The best partners are small to mid-size agencies that already serve brands with several physical locations and have no listing management offer of their own.

Good fit
  • Digital marketing, SEO and web design agencies with 5 or more multi-location clients
  • Agencies that already sell social media or Google Ads and want a recurring add-on
  • Teams of 5 to 50 people, with an account manager who owns client reporting
Poor fit
  • Freelancers with fewer than 3 clients
  • Agencies that compete on the lowest price

Partner fit check

Use the qualifying questions on a first call. Pick an answer for each and the verdict updates as you go.

1. How many of your clients have more than 5 locations?
2. Do you manage their Google Business Profiles today, and how?
3. Who would own this service internally?
4. Do your clients ask for monthly performance reports?
5. How many active clients do you have, and how do you usually win them?
Verdict
Answer the questions

Five questions, about two minutes on a call.
