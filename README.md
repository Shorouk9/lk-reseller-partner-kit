# Reseller Partner Kit

An independent proposal by Shorouk for a white-label reseller program for Local Knowledge: agencies sell listing management and Brand SoLoMo reporting under their own brand, so Local Knowledge can reach North America and Southeast Asia without building local sales teams.

**Live page:** https://shorouk9.github.io/lk-reseller-partner-kit/

## What's inside

- **Ideal partner profile** with an interactive **partner fit check**: five qualifying questions that give a Good fit / Worth a second call / Poor fit verdict.
- **30-day partner onboarding**: a week-by-week checklist from signing to the first client's first report.
- **Pricing model**: wholesale per location, partner-set resale price, and the decisions still open.
- **Reseller earnings calculator**: clients, locations per client, wholesale and resale price in; monthly and yearly partner margin, client billing, margin per client and Local Knowledge revenue out.
- **Partner success metrics**: four health numbers with proposed targets, plus a 60-day rescue rule.
- **Partner sales sheet**: a one-page draft with a logo slot, printable to PDF.
- **Open questions** to settle before launch.

## About

All prices and targets are placeholders. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Local Knowledge.

## Tech

A single self-contained `index.html` with no build step, hosted on GitHub Pages. Nothing entered on the page leaves the visitor's browser.
